Watch the *order* these four lines appear in. [///]

2:12 — SHOT 8 · OWNERSHIP AND THE RULE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Four people built this, and ownership runs by *package*, not by file. [//]
Two engineers editing different methods in one file still collide. Two
engineers editing different packages [//] essentially never do. [///]

It validates nothing. It stores nothing. It prices nothing. [//]
